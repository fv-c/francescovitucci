# Biography

Francesco Vitucci, born in 1991 and recipient of the 2022 "Goffredo Petrassi" Award from the President of the Italian Republic Sergio Mattarella, is a composer from Bari.

## Education

In 2016 he obtained the First Level Academic Diploma in "Composition" with full marks and honours, under the guidance of Biagio Putignano, at the "Niccolò Piccinni" Conservatory in Bari. With the same teacher and at the same institution, he attended the experimental Second Level two-year programme in "Musical disciplines with a technological focus - Composition", obtaining the diploma in 2018 with full marks and a special mention from the committee.

In May 2020 he obtained the Master of Advanced Studies in "Music Composition" with full marks and honours at the Conservatory of Italian Switzerland in Lugano, under the guidance of Nadir Vassena.

In June 2022, under the guidance of Ivan Fedele, he obtained the Advanced Specialization Diploma in "Composition" at the Accademia Nazionale di Santa Cecilia in Rome, with full marks and honours. At the same institution, he attended the "Electronic Music" course taught by Michelangelo Lupone.

In July 2024, under the guidance of Francesco Scagliola, he obtained the Second Level Academic Diploma in Electronic Music with full marks, honours and academic mention at the "Niccolò Piccinni" Conservatory in Bari. He is currently a PhD candidate at the same institution in "Computational electroacoustic music composition, generative algorithmic models and computational complexity".

Among the artists with whom he has furthered his studies in courses and masterclasses, in Italy and abroad, are Reinhard Febel, Julian Anderson, Luigi Ceccarelli, Carlo Infante, Elio Martusciello, Riccardo Santoboni, Daniela Terranova, Maurizio Azzan, Zeno Baldi, Giulia Lorusso, Anssi Karttunen, Steven Dann and Lisa Fornhammar.

## Awards and Distinctions

His works have been positively evaluated in several national and international composition competitions.

In November 2015 he took part in the First International Composition Competition "Music and Poetry between Italy and Japan", organized by the Italian Cultural Institute in Osaka, winning publication of the piece "Respiri erosi" for soprano, flute and guitar.

In 2017 his composition "Tryptich of human sound", for violin, cello and piano, received an extraordinary mention from the jury of the third edition of the "Vittorio Fellegara" International Composition Competition. His "5 haiku" for voice and cello were among the finalists of the International Call for Scores - Musica Prospettiva 2019-20.

In March 2021, "Triptych", for flute, clarinet, violin, cello and piano, won the "Coloana infinitului" International Composition Competition in Romania. In the same year he won First Prize in the "2 Agosto" International Composition Competition with "Nelle pieghe" for orchestra, while "Stella" received both the "Best piece of the trilogy" award and the "Best piece among the youngest composers" award.

In May 2022, "Neve / Schnee", for soprano and piano on a text by Tilo Krause, won both the Jurypreis and the Publikumpreis at the "Junges Lied-Wettbewerb" competition in Zurich. In October 2022 he received the Grand Prix at the 4th International Composer Competition "New Music Generation 2022", organized by the Kazakh National University of Arts.

In January 2023 he was a finalist in the "Luciano Berio" International Composition Competition. His piece "A deep breath", for quadraphonic digital fixed media and live electronics on S.T.ON3L, was selected for the International Computer Music Conference 2024, held at Hanyang University in Seoul, where it was presented in its world premiere.

In October 2024 he received the Goffredo Petrassi Award from the President of the Italian Republic Sergio Mattarella as the best student of the Composition Course at the Accademia Nazionale di Santa Cecilia for the year 2022.

## Artistic and Concert Activity

An ordinary member of S.I.M.C. (Italian Society of Contemporary Music), his works are performed in concerts and contemporary music festivals in Italy and abroad. Among the most significant occasions are the staging of the ballet "La Tempesta", the result of a collaboration with the University of California Irvine, and several performances in the United States and Croatia.

As a member of the EGCAM group for applied music, in 2015 he created a sonorization for the Contemporary Sacred Art festival "Pro Arte Pro Deo" in Monteroni di Lecce, presenting the works "Di luce e attimi lontani" and "Istanti d'ombra". He also took part in the multimedia project "Comporre insieme", promoted by S.I.M.C. and L'Artificio, presenting "Imprinting" in Reggio Emilia in December 2018.

He has collaborated several times with soloists, ensembles and orchestras from the institutions where he studied. For the Orchestra of the "Niccolò Piccinni" Conservatory in Bari he wrote "Variazioni" for orchestra, performed in 2016, and "Daedalus" for orchestra and fixed electronics, performed in 2018. In May 2019 the world premiere of "Hex-A" for string sextet took place, entrusted to the soloists of the Accademia d'Archi Arrigoni.

Recent premieres include "Pinax" for solo violin at Teatro Studio Borgna in Rome in May 2021, "Corpora" for eleven instruments and "In the middle of" for two performers and electroacoustic devices in June 2022, "Cartigli" for piano and ensemble in Ferrara in November 2022, "Projections" for string trio and live electronics at ArteScienza 2023 in Rome, and "Preludio ai riti del fuoco", commissioned by the ICO Symphony Orchestra of the Metropolitan City of Bari, in March 2024.

Two commissions are dedicated to the same concert institution: "Imago Hominis Myrae" (2025) for narrator, orchestra and fixed media - a work centred on the cult of Saint Nicholas and connected to the broader IHM project for the artificial reconstruction of memories of historical figures - premiered in September 2025 at the Teatro Piccinni in Bari; and "Del quieto smarrirsi" (2026) for orchestra, after Henry Purcell's "Chaconne in G minor" Z. 730, premiered for the opening of the Bari Metropolitan Orchestra's 2026 season.

"Innesto: Habitat della Memoria" (2025), for choir, percussion ensemble and orchestra, was written for the ICO Fondazione Orchestra Sinfonica di Sanremo and premiered in December 2025 at the Teatro Centrale in Sanremo.

Among his most frequently performed compositions is "(ec)stasis" for marimba, vibraphone, multi-percussion and live electronics, which in 2024 was performed in six concerts in South America, followed by a further performance for Notti Sacre 2025 at the Auditorium Diocesano Vallisa in Bari.

## Artistic Residencies

Among his recent artistic residencies, "Mousike et Arete" stands out: promoted by the Italian Ministry for Cultural Heritage and Activities in collaboration with Orchestra della Magna Grecia and ArCoPu, it led him to compose "Taras", a cantata for baritone, choir and orchestra dedicated to the city of Taranto.

He was also a guest in Saint-Etienne in May 2021 for the Journees Art & Science 2021, where he presented "At the origin of" for saxophone and Sampo, and in 2022 he took part in the "Creative Dialogue France" artistic residency programme at the Chateau de Beauchene in Champtoce-sur-Loire, where he created "Sospira l'acqua".

## Other Activities

In October 2020 the Puglia Region awarded him a professional qualification certificate as "Head of musical composition and writing", a recognition that enabled him to begin collaborations with the Paolo Grassi Foundation in Martina Franca.

Since November 2020 he has been responsible for the Music Analysis seminar for students of the Master of Arts in Orchestral Conducting at the Conservatory of Italian Switzerland. In April 2021, at the same institution, he collaborated with Nadir Vassena as assistant to the Algorithmic Composition course.

He has taught Music Technologies in several upper secondary schools. Between 2023 and 2024 he was a Composition teacher at the "G. Rossini" Conservatory in Pesaro.
